Activities · drag into calendar

Drag an activity onto the schedule, or click and drag an empty section of the grid to draw a time block.

Saved schedule

Your blocks save locally in this browser. Export JSON to move them to another device or keep a backup.

Planner settings

Week starts Monday. Time is shown in your computer's timezone.

How to use

Create: click-drag empty calendar space, or drag an activity from the sidebar.
Move: drag an existing block.
Resize: drag its lower edge.
Edit: double-click a block.
Repeat: copy last week's blocks forward, keeping the same times.
Precision: times snap to 15 minutes.
